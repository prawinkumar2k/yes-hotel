# YES HOTELS — DEMO SCRIPT

*Do not show the audience the gap matrix first.*

Start with:
> **"Welcome to YES HOTELS, a complete hotel management system designed to manage the hotel's daily operations from one place."**

---

### 1. Manager Dashboard
> **"This is the manager's dashboard. It gives us a quick view of today's hotel operations."**

*(Point to: Today's arrivals, Today's departures, Occupied rooms, Available rooms, Revenue, Payments, Housekeeping, Maintenance)*

---

### 2. Create Booking
> **"Let's create a booking for a new guest."**

*(Enter: Guest name, Phone, Stay dates, Number of guests, Room)*

Then:
> **"The system checks the room availability before confirming the booking."**

---

### 3. Check In
> **"The guest has arrived, so we can now check them in."**

*(Show: Guest details, Room, Identity verification, Stay details)*

Then:
> **"Once the guest is checked in, the room automatically becomes occupied and the guest bill is created."**

---

### 4. Guest Bill
> **"This is the guest's bill. Room charges and applicable taxes are calculated automatically."**

*(Add a payment)*
> **"The guest can make a cash, card, or Unified Payments Interface payment."**

---

### 5. Restaurant
> **"The guest can also use the hotel restaurant. The restaurant order can be linked to the guest's room."**

*(Create an order)*

Then:
> **"The restaurant team receives the order, prepares it, and the amount can be added to the guest's bill."**

---

### 6. Checkout
> **"When the guest is ready to leave, the receptionist can review the final bill and complete the checkout."**

Then:
> **"After checkout, the room is not immediately sold to another guest. It first goes to housekeeping."**

*(This is a very good feature to demonstrate.)*

---

### 7. Housekeeping
*(Switch to housekeeping)*

> **"The housekeeping team can see that this room needs cleaning."**

*(Start cleaning. Complete cleaning. Then inspection.)*

> **"After cleaning, a supervisor can inspect the room before it becomes available again."**

*(Release the room)*

> **"Now the room is ready for the next guest."**

---

### 8. Cashier
*(Show cashier)*

> **"The cashier can see the money received during the day and manage the current cashier shift."**

*(Show: Cash, Card, Unified Payments Interface, Advances, Refunds, Closing balance)*

---

### 9. Reports
> **"Management can review the hotel's daily sales and cash position from the reporting section."**

*(Show: Daily Sales Summary and Cash Sheet)*

---

### 10. Security Demo (Optional)
> **"Different employees see different information based on their responsibilities."**

*(Show Manager Dashboard, then try an unauthorized page)*

> **"The system prevents users from accessing information outside their responsibility."**

---

### 11. Closing Statement
> **"YES HOTELS brings reservations, front desk operations, rooms, housekeeping, guest billing, payments, restaurant operations, maintenance and management reporting into one system. The goal is to give hotel management one clear place to understand what is happening across the property and what requires attention."**

*(Stop here. Don't start explaining unfinished ERP modules unless they ask.)*

If they ask "Is everything completed?", say:
> **"The core hotel operations are ready for demonstration. We are currently completing deeper financial reconciliation, multi-property edge-case validation and several advanced enterprise modules such as payroll, laundry, spa, advanced revenue management and artificial intelligence."**
